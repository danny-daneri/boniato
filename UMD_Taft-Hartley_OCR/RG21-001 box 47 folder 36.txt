TO ALL LOCAL UNIONS
IN THE UNITED STATES.
August 21, 1947
Dear Sirs and Brothers:
Local unions are asking me many questions about the
Taft-Hartley law.
I will try, to the best of my ability, to give the lo-
cal unions the information they need to function under this law.
To reiterate what I have already written to our local
unions about the Taft-Hartley law:
Local unions that have agreements signed prior to Aug-
ust 22nd of this year will not have to worry very much about the
Taft-Hartley law until the time arrives for renewing thir agree-
ments next year.
However, in the months ahead of us, we must prepare our-
selves to comply with the requirements of the law in order to
petition the National Labor Relations Board for union shop elec-
tions when our agreements come up for renewal next year•
At the proper time I will send each local union a copy
of the form that has to be filed with the Office for the Regis-
tration of Labor Organizations, United States Department of Labor,
Washington, D. C.
I will also send you forms prepared by our Research De-
