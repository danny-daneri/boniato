Dear Mr. President:
We are informed that under date of July 8 President
William Green of the American Federation of Labor addressed a
communication to you concerning the failure
of the congress to
include in the War Department appropriation bill for 1948 the
usual clause prohibiting speed up systems and bonus practices.
Such a provision has been included in appropriation bills for
both the ler and Navy Departments for many years and railway
labor has consistently Joined with other labor groups in urging
that these vicious practices and systems be prohibited in the
operations of these two departments of our Government.
We join the American Federation of Labor in respect-
fully asking that an Executive Order be issued which would
apply to the War Department and which would forbid the inaugura-
tion of any speed up or bonus systen such as the highly objection-
able Taylor System. We are informed that the Navy Department
appropriation bill contains such a prohibition and we believe that
the same rule should apply to both Departments.
we are familiar with the contents of the letter sent you
on June 6 by Mr. N. P. Alifas of the International Association of
Machinists and we fully endorse his statements.
Respectfully yours,
