"The open shop is represented as the embodiment of a great
principle - the principle of equal rights and equal opportunity.
The Declaration of Independence, the Constitution, the Bible,
are quoted in support of the open shop. Organized labor 1s
denounced in the bitterest terms for daring to demand so

work.
It is fundamental. To give it up would be to restore
salvery and to make slaves of the nost skilled and competent of
American workmen.
And why should not the union man work with and beside the non-
That, frankly, is none of the employers business.
Labor is under no obligation to justify its likes and dislikes to
We were constantly told that supply and demand regulated
the enploy ent of labor and that the market was free and should
This being the case (we grant it for the arguments sake)
The worknen may say to the employer that he will not work for him

the employers part, to
engage no men obnoxious to him.
I'he union closed shop is an American institution - as American
as the Clothing Manufacturers Association. The law of the land gives
the employer the right to hire any labor he can get. The law does
not give the employer the freedon to impress workmen, to enslave
them, to drag them into his factory on any terms he chooses to granti
then
in the excercise of his "berevolence". He has the right to
bid, to make a contract. But the workman has the same rights.
Now since every man has the right to sell his labor as he sees
fit, he has the right not to sell it to the employer who wants
an open shop.
A manufacturer may buy all his raw material, all his machinery,
from one company. No one is idiotic enough to
tell him that he
must patronize a dozen difference companies. Why may he not buy
all his labor of one union?
The union shop is not contrary to public policy because
public policy demands above all things the preservation of the
rights of contract.
It is true that the "closed" or union shop
contract, like contracts for the supply of raw material, machinery,
etc., by particular persons, "tends to create limited, partial
monopolies," but the law never has and does not now penetrate again
"limited partial monopolies".
In short decisions against the union or "closed" shop are
founded on ignorant or prejudiced perversions of all contract law
and fundamental individual rights.
