3 Territories hes been complied by the Division of Labor Standarda, U. S.
Depurtment of Lebor, Willien L. Comolly, director, announced today•
These will guide unions and management in meeting requirements of the
Taft-Hertley Act to notify State mediation agencies simultaneously with
the Federal Mediation and Conciliation Service of disputes over the terni-
nation or modification of existing collective bargaining contracts cover-
ing employees in an industry affecting commerce.
"Under the Labor Menagement Relations Act of 1947," Mr. Connolly
explained, "either party to such a contract proposing termination or
change therein must notify the other party 60 days prior to the time that
the proposal would take effect. If a dispute arising over such a proposal
is not settled within 30 days, then the Act requires that the State medi-
ation agency in the State in which the dispute occurs must be notified of
its existence simultaneously with the Federal Madiation and Conciliation
Service. In general strikes and lockouts are forbidden during the entire
60-day notice period."
